# Sources — Paws & Hearts PH

Every organization in `data/organizations.json` was found through public web research and
confirmed against one of the sources listed below. The `source_url` field on each entry
points at the primary source used to verify that specific listing.

**Verification date for this release: 2026-09-30.**

If a field could not be verified from a source, it was set to `null` (email,
requirements) or `""` (website, facebook) rather than guessed.

## Access notes (links that are live but block automated checks)

These URLs returned a non-200 code to our scripted checks but are **not** broken — they
simply refuse non-browser traffic. Both were confirmed to serve their real content when
requested the way a person's browser would.

| URL | What happens to a bot | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <https://carawelfare.ph/> and <https://carawelfare.ph/volunteer/> | Serves a Cloudflare *"Confirm you are human"* challenge (HTTP 403) before revealing the page | **Live.** The volunteer page's contents were read successfully during research (volunteer rules, e-mail, facilities), and CARA's official Facebook page returns HTTP 200 |
| <https://paws.org.ph/> and <https://paws.org.ph/volunteer/> | Aborted a `DOMContentLoaded`-timed request, then loaded normally | **Live.** Both return HTTP 200 with the correct titles *"The Philippine Animal Welfare Society • PAWS"* and *"Volunteer • The Philippine Animal Welfare Society"* |

## Notes on gaps

- **Farm-animal sanctuaries.** Several searches for a standalone Philippine farm-animal
  sanctuary with an open volunteer program returned no organization that could be verified.
  The listing that genuinely houses rescued farm animals is the
  **Philippine Animal Rescue Team (PART)**, confirmed by the organization's own posts, and
  it is the entry filed under the `farm` category.
- **Contact details.** Where an organization published no email address, `email` is `null`.
  Where an organization had no official website of its own, `website` is `""` and the
  Facebook page is used instead.
- **Volunteer availability.** Some organizations (for example CARA) state publicly that
  general sign-ups are temporarily closed. This is recorded in `how_to_join` rather than
  the entry being hidden — you will want to ask directly either way.
